Rewrites that stay true to your listing

Persuasive copy is easy. Copy that doesn’t invent things is the hard part. Every rewrite goes through two checks before it reaches you: fixed rules, then a second model pass that compares it against your original.

  • No new numbers or specifications
  • No materials or claims your source didn’t state
  • No medical or guarantee claims
  • Titles within 80 characters, descriptions within 1,000
  • Spreadsheet-safe output, with formulas neutralized

Questions

What happens to rows that fail the checks?

They come back exactly as you sent them, marked unchanged_needs_review, so you never receive an unverified rewrite by accident.

Which marketplaces does it work for?

It rewrites plain titles and descriptions, so the output works anywhere you can paste text or import a CSV. It doesn’t connect to any marketplace account.

Is my data kept?

Uploads, results and your email are deleted automatically 14 days after your order. Unpaid uploads are deleted after 24 hours. Your listing text is sent to our AI provider, Anthropic, to produce the rewrite.

Can I trust the output without reading it?

No. The checks catch invented specs and risky claims, but the output is machine-generated, so review it before you publish.

Something went wrong with my order.

Contact us within 14 days using the address on the refund policy page and we’ll review it.
